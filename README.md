## 🌍 About This Project

Academic tools should be accessible to everyone.

Unfortunately, not every student, researcher, independent scholar, or academic can afford a paid Overleaf subscription.

To help with this, I host my own instance of **Overleaf Community Edition** and provide **free accounts** to people who genuinely need access to an online LaTeX environment.

The goal is simple:

> **Give students and researchers access to a useful collaborative LaTeX environment regardless of their financial situation.**

There is **no account fee**, **no subscription charge**, and **no payment required**.

---

## 🚀 What Is Available?

The service runs on a privately hosted installation of:

### Overleaf Community Edition

You can use it for writing and managing LaTeX projects through a web browser.

It provides an Overleaf-style workflow suitable for:

* 📚 Research papers
* 🎓 Theses and dissertations
* 📝 University assignments
* 📄 Academic reports
* 📊 Scientific documents
* 📑 Journal manuscripts
* 📖 Books and lecture notes
* 🧪 Research documentation
* 📐 Mathematical documents
* 🤝 Collaborative LaTeX editing

The experience is intended to be similar to using Overleaf for normal academic LaTeX work.

> **Important:** This is a self-hosted Overleaf Community Edition service. It is not an official Overleaf Premium/Professional subscription and some features available in commercial Overleaf plans may not be available.

# 🔐 Privacy

Privacy is important for academic and research work.

I am also a researcher, and I understand that unpublished papers, research ideas, manuscripts, theses, experiments, and academic documents may contain confidential or sensitive information.

My intention is to operate this service with respect for user privacy.

### Privacy principles

* Your projects are not sold to third parties.
* Your personal information is not sold for advertising.
* Your account information is used for operating the service.
* Users should only provide the minimum information required for account creation.
* Administrative access is limited to what is necessary to operate, maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and back up the service.
* I do not intentionally inspect private user projects without an operational, security, abuse-prevention, or user-support reason.

However, please understand that this is an independently operated community service.

### ⚠️ Important Privacy Notice

No online service can honestly guarantee absolute privacy or zero risk.

For extremely confidential, legally protected, commercially sensitive, classified, medical, or otherwise high-risk documents, you should use an infrastructure and security environment specifically approved for that type of information.

Research and education should not be limited by someone's ability to pay for software.

A researcher may have the skills to produce excellent work but still lack access to commercial academic tools.

A student may need LaTeX for a thesis but may not have access to an institutional Overleaf subscription.

An independent researcher may simply need somewhere reliable to write and compile LaTeX documents.

If I already have the infrastructure available, I would rather allow others to benefit from it.

This project is my small contribution toward:

# 💾 Keep Your Own Backups

Although reasonable efforts may be made to maintain the server and protect user projects, this is a free independently operated service.

**Always maintain your own backup of important LaTeX projects.**

For important academic work, keep copies on:

* Your computer
* Git
* GitHub/GitLab where appropriate
* External storage
* Institutional storage
* Another trusted backup service

Never keep the only copy of an important thesis, manuscript, or research project on any single online service.

# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

## Is it really free?

Yes.

Accounts are provided without a subscription fee for users who need the service.

---

## Is this the official Overleaf website?

No.

This is an **independently operated, self-hosted instance of Overleaf Community Edition**.

It is not operated by, sponsored by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the official Overleaf service unless explicitly stated otherwise.

---

## Is it exactly the same as Overleaf Premium/Professional?

Not necessarily.

The interface and normal LaTeX workflow may feel familiar, but Overleaf Community Edition and commercial Overleaf subscriptions can have different features.

This project should therefore be considered a **free self-hosted alternative for common LaTeX workflows**, not a commercial Overleaf subscription.
